The purchase is a launch plan.

TraceLite is a fictional tool that turns HTTP logs into a local timeline. Its solo builder has five hours, no customers and no advertising budget. What should the builder do in the first week?

The promise has three criteria.

  • Relevance: use the product, audience and available resources.
  • Actionability: give practical steps and a way to collect useful feedback.
  • Grounding: avoid invented capabilities, customers or results.

A generic answer can mention the product and still fail the purpose. The court judges that disagreement.

The court judges. The seller pays.

GenLayer provides independent adjudication. A trusted registrar admits payment and delivery evidence, and a seller-operated worker applies the finalized ruling. This demo does not guarantee repayment by an unwilling or insolvent seller.

The final case viewer will be read-only. Buyer keys stay with the agent; the public page cannot initiate a funded purchase or refund.

Every component has a completion check.

The blueprint covers signatures, payment recovery, evidence, storage races, court consensus, finality, refund replay protection, the skill, interface, tests, deployment, costs and the video.
