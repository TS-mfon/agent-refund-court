# Art direction and provenance brief

Audience: Internet Court and its x402 partner reviewers. Surface: a 44-second horizontal demo, 1920×1080 at 30 FPS. The desired action is to run the public agent skill and inspect the original receipts.

Source of truth is the public completed testnet case and actual browser captures. AI assistance authors the Remotion edit, motion, typography and captions. No generative image/video provider is used.

## Still-frame design brief used for implementation

A white and cobalt evidence studio. Large plain-language text on the left, actual product evidence on the right. Preserve the three authentic protocol logos and their proportions. Show one concrete economic consequence: the exact USDC principal returns to the original buyer. Use quiet shadows and a single consistent blue; avoid decorative generated imagery. Keep the recorded-case and fictional-brief/delivery-fixture disclosure visible.

## Motion brief used for implementation

Produce a 44-second horizontal captioned video in eight scenes. Open with the question an agent asks after paying for work. Show the actual agent command and its bounded brief, then the payment receipt and original weak answer. Reveal an exact excerpt and the signed buyer complaint. Connect frozen evidence to the finalized independent GenLayer ruling. Show the cooperating seller's real refund and line up the original payer and principal. End with the public viewer and repository.

Camera movement is a restrained crop/zoom on real UI captures. Motion is deterministic and derived from frame numbers: short eased reveals, tight scene cuts, and a readable final hold. Audio is intentionally silent. No generated footage, synthetic receipts, invented quotations, or claims of guaranteed repayment.

## Export QA

The renderer rejects unverified evidence before export. It checks the resulting MP4's actual movie duration, video dimensions and sample count, decodes the complete file through bundled FFmpeg, preserves the previous public export until the new render succeeds, and records a SHA-256 fingerprint. Inspect opening, scene boundaries and final frames before submission.

The exact receipts, capture timestamp, installed Remotion version, source hashes and output fingerprint are written to provenance.json only after successful rendering. Seeds are not applicable to the frame-derived composition.
